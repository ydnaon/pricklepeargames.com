Frequently asked

How do I back up or move my progress?

Open Settings → Save Code. It copies your progress to your clipboard as a text code you can paste somewhere safe or into the game on another device. The code holds game stats only — no personal information — and nothing is ever sent off your device.

What is the Endless unlock?

The game is free to play through the first MEGA DOODLE. One optional in-app purchase ($2.99) unlocks Endless mode so you can keep going. There are no ads and no other purchases.

I bought Endless but it's not showing / I got a new phone.

Reinstall the game and use your store's Restore Purchases option, signed in with the same account you bought it on. If it still doesn't appear, email us and we'll sort it out.

Does the game collect any of my data?

No. Doodle Defenders is offline and single-player — no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The only thing it talks to is your app store, to check whether you own the Endless unlock. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

What permissions does the app use?

Vibrate (haptic feedback), Wake lock (keeps the screen awake during a run), and on Android the billing permission Google's library adds so we can offer the Endless unlock. Nothing else.
