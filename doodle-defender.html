The page is alive

Your doodle fights. You just move.

The doodles have escaped. You steer one doodle around a single sheet of notebook paper while waves of hand-drawn shapes chase you down.

You never aim and never fire — that happens on its own. What you choose is the build: every level, take a new weapon or another level on one you have. Max out two and you can fuse them into one stronger weapon.

Pencil to boss fight

One run, start to finish

01

Pick your doodle

Nine doodles, each with its own starting weapon and stat line. Skip, Flick and Tubs are there from the first run; the other six unlock as you build fusions.

02

Hold the center

Your whole job is not getting hit. Weave the gaps, drag the swarm behind you, and stay alive as the waves get meaner.

03

Level up, then fuse

Each level puts up a hand of three cards: a new weapon, or another level on one you own. Take two weapons to max and a fusion card appears — it merges them into a single stronger weapon and frees the slot.

04

Reach seven minutes

Enemies scale with the clock the whole way. At 7:00 the HUD swaps to a boss bar and the first MEGA DOODLE lands on the page.

At seven minutes

☠ MEGA DOODLE I ☠

The camera pans off your doodle and it erupts onto the page in a burst of ink. Everything you drafted and fused over those seven minutes is what you have to take it down with.

Beat it and it shatters into ink — then MEGA DOODLE II is three minutes out. Then III, and on up the ladder. The numeral you reach is the score that actually counts.
