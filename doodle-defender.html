The page is alive

The doodles have escaped. You hold the center of a notebook sheet while ever-bigger waves of hand-drawn shapes scribble their way toward you.

Your doodle fights on its own — you just move. Dodge, survive, and every few seconds draft a new weapon, level it up, and fuse pairs into over-the-top ultimates. Last long enough and the whole sheet tears open.
