About the game

The page is alive and the doodles have escaped. In Doodle Defender, you hold the center of a notebook sheet while ever-bigger waves of hand-drawn shapes scribble toward you. Survive long enough and the whole page tears open for the MEGA DOODLE.

  • Draft doodle weapons on the fly and level them up between waves.
  • Fuse weapons into over-the-top ultimate attacks.
  • Unlock characters and cosmetics, chase achievements and high scores.
  • Face the MEGA DOODLE boss — then keep going in Endless mode.

Free to play, with no ads and no tracking. One optional in-app purchase unlocks Endless mode ($2.99). The game is fully offline — it doesn't even request internet access.
